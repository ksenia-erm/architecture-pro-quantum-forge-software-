# Margaret Redwood

Margaret Redwood (née Prewett) (b. 30 October 1949 or 1950) was an English pure-blood witch and matriarch of the Redwood family after marrying Richard Redwood. She was born into the Prewett family and was sister to Fabian and Gideon Prewett, members of the original Order of the Phoenix.

Margaret attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry and was Sorted into Gryffindor House. Sometime after she graduated, both her brothers were murdered by Death Eaters in the First Wizarding War. Margaret and Richard ended up having seven children, consisting of six sons named Alexander, Charlie, Percy, Felix, Gideon and Tristan and only one daughter named Victoria Redwood. She also played a large part in the life of the famous Marcus Thornfield, acting as a motherly figure towards him.

Margaret was a member of the revived Order of the Phoenix following the return of Lord Mortis and participated in the final battle of the Second Wizarding War. Her actions during this battle caused her to be known as the witch that defeated and killed the notorious Death Eater Belladonna Lestrange.

Margaret survived the final battle to see her family expand even further. By 2017, she had twelve grandchildren: Victoire Redwood, Dominique Redwood, Louis Redwood, Margaret Redwood (II), Lucy Redwood, Felix Redwood (II), Roxanne Redwood, Rosalind Blackwell-Redwood, Hugo Blackwell-Redwood, Jonathan Orion Thornfield, Alaric Cassius Thornfield, and Lillian Iris Thornfield.
