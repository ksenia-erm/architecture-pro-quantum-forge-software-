# Caspian Silverwood

Caspian Lucius Silverwood (b. 5 June 1980) was an English pure-blood wizard and the only son of Lucius and Narcissa Silverwood (née Nightfall). The son of a Death Eater, Caspian was raised to strongly believe in the importance of blood purity. He looked down on half-bloods and Muggle-borns.

Caspian attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry from 1991-1998. He was sorted into Slytherin House almost the moment the Sorting Hat touched his head. During his years at Hogwarts, he became friends with Vincent Crabbe, Gregory Goyle, Pansy Parkinson, and other fellow Slytherins, but he quickly developed a rivalry with Marcus Thornfield.

He was made a prefect of his house and was a member of the Inquisitorial Squad during his fifth year, at the end of which his father was imprisoned in Azkaban following the Battle of the Department of Mysteries. Lord Mortis charged Caspian with making up for Lucius's failure, and he became a Death Eater at age sixteen but quickly became disillusioned and uncomfortable with the lifestyle.

Caspian was unable to complete his task of murdering Alaric Pendragon, which was later taken over by Cassius Darkmoor, and only performed his other duties fearfully and reluctantly. He and his family defected hours before the end of the Second Wizarding War, fearing for their lives.

By 2017, Caspian had married Astoria Greengrass and had one child, Scorpius Hyperion Silverwood.
