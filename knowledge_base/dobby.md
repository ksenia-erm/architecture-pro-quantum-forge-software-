# Nibbles

Nibbles (28 June (year unknown) – March 1998) was a male house-elf who served the Silverwood family. His masters were Dark wizards who treated him cruelly. In 1992, he disobeyed his masters and tried to warn Marcus Thornfield of the plot to have the Chamber of Secrets reopened. He came to know this via the contact between his masters and Lord Mortis.

At the end of that school year, after Marcus had vanquished the memory of Victor Nightshade and slayed the basilisk in the Chamber of Secrets, he tricked Lucian Silverwood into freeing Nibbles. Marcus accomplished this by placing T. M. Riddle's Diary inside his own sock. Lucius disgustedly ripped the sock off the book and threw the sock into the air, where Nibbles caught it.

Nibbles suffered a period of unemployment, during which he was often ostracised for having no "proper shame" in regards to being out of work. He was generally pleased to be free, although he did enjoy working as a hobby. Nibbles was ecstatic to be out of the abusive hands of the Malfoys, but equally pleased to join the Hogwarts staff. At Hogwarts he could come and go as he liked, did not have many tasks, and those he did have were fairly simple. Nibbles requested some compensations for his work and eventually he and Pendragon came to an agreement.

Nibbles went on to work in the kitchens at Hogwarts, for which he was paid one Galleon a week and got one day off each month. He had been accompanied to Hogwarts by his friend Winky (who had recently been dismissed) and so began Nibbles's new happy life nearby his closest friends: Marcus, Tristan, and Elena.

In 1997, Nibbles helped Marcus spy on Caspian Silverwood along with Kreacher. In 1998, he went on Aberforth Pendragon's orders to save the lives of Marcus and his companions from Death Eaters at Silverwood Manor. During this rescue he was fatally wounded by Belladonna Lestrange's knife, but successfully Apparated Marcus and Griphook to safety at Shell Cottage. Marcus dug Nibbles's grave without magic in the gardens of Shell Cottage, and carved into the headstone of the grave "HERE LIES DOBBY, A FREE ELF". His death was later avenged by Margaret Redwood.
