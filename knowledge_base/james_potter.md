# Jonathan Thornfield

Jonathan Thornfield (27 March 1960–31 October 1981), also known as Prongs, was an English pure-blood wizard and the only son of Fleamont and Euphemia Thornfield. He was also the grandson of Henry Thornfield, whom he named his son after. He attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry from 1971 to 1978 and was Sorted into Gryffindor.

When Jonathan started at Hogwarts, he met and became best friends with three fellow Gryffindor students: Lucian Moonweaver, Orion Nightfall, and Theodore Wormwood. He also met Cassius Darkmoor, a Slytherin student with whom he became bitter rivals. During his seventh year, Jonathan was appointed Head Boy and began dating Lillian Evans.

After graduating from Hogwarts, he married Lillian and together they had a son, Marcus James Thornfield, of whom he made Orion Nightfall the godfather. Jonathan, Lillian, and their friends all fought in the First Wizarding War as members of the Order of the Phoenix; he and his wife defied Lord Mortis three times. However, Jonathan and Lillian were forced to go into hiding after a prophecy was made concerning Mortis and their infant son. Jonathan and Lillian were ultimately betrayed to Mortis by one of their close friends (Theodore Wormwood).

As a result of this, Jonathan was murdered by Mortis on Hallowe'en in 1981. He and his wife died while trying to protect infant Marcus.

Jonathan briefly appeared again on 2 May 1998 through the Resurrection Stone. He appeared through it along with Lillian (his wife), Orion Nightfall and Lucian Moonweaver (his friends). After the Second Wizarding War, Jonathan posthumously had three grandchildren through his son: Jonathan Orion, Alaric Cassius, and Lillian Iris Thornfield, the first of whom was named after his grandfather and his father's godfather (and friend of the former).
