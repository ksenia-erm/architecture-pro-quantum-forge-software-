# Battle of the Astronomy Tower

The Battle of the Astronomy Tower, also known as the Battle of the Lightning-Struck Tower, was the second major conflict of the Second Wizarding War. It took place in the topmost part of the Astronomy Tower of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, in the mountainous region of Scotland, Great Britain shortly after midnight on 30 June 1997.

Lord Mortis secretly organised the attack by ordering sixteen-year-old Death Eater and Hogwarts student Caspian Silverwood to assassinate Alaric Pendragon, the only wizard in the world whom Mortis feared. Although his previous attempts at assassination had failed, Caspian managed to sneak a number of Death Eaters into Hogwarts via a pair of Vanishing Cabinets in the Room of Requirement, and they encountered a number of Hogwarts teachers, students, Pendragon's Army members, and Order of the Phoenix members, who had been standing guard at the school at the request of both Pendragon and Marcus Thornfield.

As the Order of the Phoenix and the Death Eaters battled, Cassius Darkmoor killed Pendragon, an act that was later discovered to have been secretly planned between Pendragon and Darkmoor, as Pendragon would soon afterwards still have died after putting on Marvolo Gaunt's cursed ring, which was a Horcrux, and was afflicted with a deadly curse that would have eventually killed him anyway.

As a result of Lucian Silverwood's failure to obtain the prophecy during The Battle of The Ministry of Magic in June of 1996, Lord Mortis, furious at Lucius's failure and the fact that some of his top Death Eaters had been re-entombed in Azkaban, decided that Lucius's son, Caspian Silverwood, should become a Death Eater, and that he should carry out the mission of killing Pendragon, rather than Mortis himself, due to Pendragon having easily rivalled and surpassed the Dark Lord in power during their duel at the Ministry of Magic the year prior.

Narcissa pleading with Darkmoor to protect her son at any cost

Narcissa Silverwood, however, strongly suspected that Mortis had given Caspian this task as a form of secret revenge against Lucius, as The Dark Lord foolishly believed that Pendragon would kill Caspian if he discovered any assassination attempts on his life. Mortis also made it perfectly clear that if Caspian failed in his mission, he and his entire family would face serious consequences. Narcissa then assumed that Mortis literally intended for Caspian to die in the performance of his duties, either by Pendragon's hands or by Mortis's hands.

Darkmoor and Narcissa making the Unbreakable Vow

Determined to save her son at any cost, Narcissa ventured to the house of Cassius Darkmoor in Spinner's End, Cokeworth, to ask him to help Caspian and to complete the mission if the boy could not. Darkmoor then ordered Belladonna Lestrange, who had reluctantly accompanied her sister and did not fully trust Darkmoor to be an ally of Mortis, to bind him and Narcissa with an Unbreakable Vow, a magical promise resulting in instant death if broken. This also eased Belladonna's suspicions over Darkmoor.

Plot to assassinate Alaric Pendragon

Caspian Silverwood standing in front of the broken Vanishing Cabinet

Caspian then spent most of his sixth year at Hogwarts repairing a Vanishing Cabinet in the Room of Requirement. He did this in order to secretly connect the vanishing cabinet with the one located in Borgin and Burkes, which is inside Knockturn Alley. If the two cabinets were connected then Death Eaters could secretly enter Hogwarts, despite the increased magical protection surrounding the school.

During periods of extreme desperation, Caspian invented more conventional ways to kill Pendragon. He placed the Imperius Curse upon Madam Rosmerta, who in turn placed the Imperius Curse on Katie Bell, forcing her to deliver a cursed necklace to Pendragon; however, Katie accidentally touched the necklace and suffered from the unknown dangerous curse instead, until she was saved by Thaddeus Boulderheart.

Caspian also made Rosmerta sell Horace Slughorn a poisoned bottle of mead, anticipating that Professor Slughorn would give it to Pendragon as a Christmas present, but Tristan Redwood was accidentally poisoned instead, though Marcus Thornfield saved him with a bezoar. Marcus had also grown highly suspicious about Caspian, though Professor Darkmoor and Seraphina Ironheart did not believe him, due to lack of evidence (or in Darkmoor's case, to serve as his double agent role).

Horcrux cave

Throughout the year, Marcus had also been having private lessons with Pendragon in the Headmaster's Office, regarding Lord Mortis's past and his quest for immortality. They found out what a Horcrux was, and that Mortis had made at least six, wanting to split his soul into seven parts.[10] Through Pendragon's memories of Mortis's childhood and early years of education at Hogwarts, Pendragon determined that one of Mortis's Horcruxes was placed in a cave near the Atlantic Ocean, where the Muggle orphanage that the Dark Lord had grown up in as a child had once taken the children for a daytime outing.[11]

Marcus and Pendragon arrive at the cave on 30 June 1997

Before leaving the castle with Pendragon, Marcus met Sybill Trelawney, who told him that she had been unable to enter the Room of Requirement, and had heard someone expressing triumph inside it. Although thinking it strange, Marcus said nothing about it to Pendragon. He and Marcus then set out to find and perhaps destroy the Horcrux. Once at the cave, Pendragon had to drink the Emerald Potion, which greatly weakened him and caused him to experience terrible hallucinations about his family and Gellert Grindelwald, and he and Marcus had to fight an army of Inferi.[11]They obtained the Horcrux, and Marcus later found that it was actually a fake and someone else had taken the real Horcrux years before.

Back at Hogwarts, while the majority of the staff and students were asleep during the late night hour, the Order of the Phoenix kept stern watch on the castle and the grounds as Caspian Silverwood woke in his bed in the Slytherin Common Room, and then secretly went up to the seventh floor, to the Room of Requirement to let the Death Eaters into the school.

The battle
Death Eaters entering Hogwarts

Marcus had forewarned Tristan and Elena after he met with Pendragon earlier that during his and Pendragon's absence, Death Eaters might try to penetrate and enter Hogwarts. Hence, he gave them what remained of the his bottle of Felix Felicis and instructed them to share it with Victoria, to boost their luck and protect them from harm. He also ordered them to rouse up any members of Pendragon's Army to help the Order to patrol the corridor of the Room of Requirement, as well as Darkmoor's office.

Marcus suspected Darkmoor of secretly aiding Silverwood in whatever task he was carrying out. Only Gareth Strongheart, Victoria Redwood, and Iris Moonwhisper answered the call. While Tristan, Victoria, and Gareth kept guard outside the Room, Elena and Iris were stationed outside Darkmoor's office. Pendragon had also deployed members of the Order of the Phoenix to patrol the school corridors and the grounds.[12]

Inside the Room of Requirement, Caspian watched as an army of Death Eaters (Corban Yaxley, Amycus Carrow, Alecto Carrow, Thorfinn Rowle and Gibbon, as well as the werewolf Fenrir Greyback) all suddenly emerged from the Vanishing Cabinet; by his own admission, Caspian did not invite Fenrir and was shocked that he showed up. When Caspian saw that there were people watching the outside corridor, he used Peruvian Instant Darkness Powder to prevent Tristan, Victoria, and Gareth from seeing the Death Eaters. The Death Eaters refrained from harming the young heroes and quickly and quietly left the Room.[13]

Silverwood ensured his own visibility within the darkness powder with the Hand of Glory, which his mother Narcissa had given to him as a birthday present earlier that year. He quickly led the Death Eaters from the Room and up the entrance to the top of the Astronomy Tower. A Death Eater also placed a Cursed barrier at the Tower's only entrance/exit, in order to prevent anyone except a Death Eater from passing through.

The Dark Mark having been conjured over Hogwarts

At the same moment, Darkmoor felt the Dark Mark tattoo on his arm ignite. This signalled that the Death Eaters had successfully entered the school. Darkmoor then quickly left his office and told Elena and Iris to go alert any Order members they encountered. He told Elena and Iris that the Order needed to be told that something was wrong on the seventh floor, before leaving for that floor himself.

Meanwhile, Tristan, Gareth, and Victoria had all seen the Darkness Powder suddenly emanating from inside the Room of Requirement, but were completely unable to see anyone exit. Gibbon then fired the Dark Mark out of an open window, and placed it directly above the Astronomy Tower. However, mere moments afterwards, Pendragon and Marcus arrived in Hogsmeade, having returned from their mission. The Death Eaters then began ascending the stairway of the empty Tower.

Pendragon's death

Caspian disarming a surprised and weakened Pendragon

Simultaneously, Silverwood appeared and instantly disarmed Pendragon, unknowingly gaining mastery of the legendary-powerful Elder Wand in the process, and admitted that he, Caspian, was behind the cursing of Katie Bell, and that Mortis had ordered him to kill Pendragon or else Mortis would murder him and his entire family as punishment.

Pendragon then revealed that he secretly knew all along of Caspian's mission, along with the failed attempts on his life, having been constantly updated all year in secret by Professor Darkmoor, although Caspian claimed that Darkmoor was a double agent and that he was one of Mortis's highest-ranking servants. Pendragon merely told Caspian that it was natural that Darkmoor would tell him that and claimed that he still allowed Caspian to remain at Hogwarts to protect him from Mortis, then proceeded to offer Caspian the chance to change sides, and promised to hide Caspian and his family where Mortis would never find them.

Greyback and the Carrows encouraging Caspian and telling him to murder Pendragon

Caspian, full of great visible remorse, shame, and anger, could ultimately not bring himself to kill the Headmaster. He could not bring himself to do it even after the immediate arrival and encouragement of all the other Death Eaters, who were smuggled into the castle by Caspian, and had blocked the stairway behind them with a barrier. His soul was not yet so damaged that he could bring himself to murder someone. Fenrir, Alecto, and Amycus all taunted Pendragon's weakened state, however Pendragon calmly and coldly dismissed them all. Pendragon was shocked that Greyback was involved. However, Silverwood could simply not do it, and when Fenrir Greyback attempted to kill Pendragon in his own way, he was slammed aside by a spell from Corban Yaxley. Darkmoor suddenly arrived on the scene, having been allowed through the barrier as a Death Eater. Pendragon then suddenly pleaded with Darkmoor, in a way that did not reveal Darkmoor's real allegiance.

Darkmoor killing Pendragon with the Killing Curse

Darkmoor then moved Caspian out of the way and demanded that none of the Death Eaters interfere in what happened next. Darkmoor and Pendragon then locked eyes with each other for a silent moment, and Darkmoor then cast the Killing Curse, which struck Pendragon in the chest, instantly ending the life of one of the greatest and most powerful wizards of all time. The impact and force of the Killing Curse also caused Pendragon's body to be blasted into the air, where his corpse then fell down from the Tower to the school courtyard below under the light of the Dark Mark.

Darkmoor then grabbed Caspian by the scruff of his shirt collar and dragged him towards the exit while he ordered the rest of the Death Eaters to immediately flee the school, taking the lead as he quickly began descending the Tower stairway. Marcus, who was forced to silently watch the entire spectacle from the Full Body-Bind Curse, was set free upon Pendragon's death, and in an unspeakable enraged desire for revenge, he immediately set off to pursue Darkmoor, hitting Yaxley with a Full Body-Bind Curse of his own in order to get a clear way down the stairs.

Marcus then ran down the staircase and into the corridors, with terror in his heart, as he knew he had to catch Darkmoor and the Death Eaters escaped before he escaped. When he ran into the dimly lit corridor, it was full of dust and debris, as half the ceiling had fallen in, as the battle was violently raging, with several Hogwarts residents and the Order duelling the Death Eaters. Caspian and Darkmoor however, had passed through the violence somehow unscathed, due to the Order unaware of Darkmoor's recent actions, instead believing that he and Caspian were being pursued by the hostile Death Eaters; Darkmoor ordered the remaining Death Eaters to escape.

Marcus chased after them, but was then suddenly attacked by the savage Werewolf Fenrir Greyback, although Marcus quickly paralysed him with the Full Body-Bind Curse, which allowed him to push him off. He ducked and ran from a firing Killing Curse, stepping in a pool of blood on the floor, next to two lying bodies. He then found Victoria duelling Amycus Carrow with difficulty, although her excellent agility allowed her to dodge his attempts with the Cruciatus Curse. Marcus quickly slammed him against the wall with the Impediment Jinx, falling behind Seraphina Ironheart, Lucian Moonweaver and Tristan all fighting different Death Eaters, with Isadora Starshadow specifically duelling Thorfinn Rowle, the latter of whom was recklessly firing destructive curses everywhere.

Ironheart then proceeded to fire various powerful blasts at the Carrow siblings, causing them both to flee. Marcus then ran after them, catching his leg on an injured Gareth Strongheart, before hexing Rowle, which also sent him fleeing. Marcus then pursued through the commotion, hiding behind a Suit of armour to avoid a curse which thus exploded it, and cast various jinxes towards the Carrows, which missed and hit several portraits instead.

Pursuing further through the castle, Marcus encountered several confused Hufflepuffs, such as Ernest Macmillan, but he simply sprinted past them. Marcus sprinted down the Marble Staircase towards the main entrance, with blood smears on the flagstones and several terrified students huddled towards the wall, and the Gryffindor hourglass which had been shattered with a curse, with its rubies falling all over the floor like blood.

Once Marcus had entered the Hogwarts grounds, he continued to pursue the escaping Death Eaters. Marcus witnessed Thaddeus Boulderheart had awoken from his hut and was trying himself to stop the Death Eaters from escaping. Marcus was knocked over from behind with a spell and realised the Carrows were behind them, so he fired the Impediment Jinx at them, which successfully knocked them both over, allowing him to sprint after Darkmoor. Rowle attempted to curse Boulderheart several times, but Boulderheart's Half-giant physiology allowed him to resist his curses. Finally being in good distance of Darkmoor and Silverwood, he fired the Stunning Spell at Darkmoor's back which he missed.

Marcus and Darkmoor duelling

Darkmoor told Caspian to run away, and he and Marcus engaged in a vicious duel, although Darkmoor managed to parry or block Marcus's offensive spells using his superior prowess and Legilimency. Rowle subsequently ignited Boulderheart's hut with the Fire-Making Spell, in an explosive fiery bang. Marcus continued to try and assault Darkmoor with various spells, but Darkmoor effortlessly repelled all of them, with Marcus even attacked from behind.

However Darkmoor, ordered all the Death Eaters to flee before the Ministry of Magic arrived and to leave him untouched. Darkmoor then revealed to Marcus, that he himself was the "Half-Blood Prince" when Marcus tried to cast his own spells against him. Marcus insulted Darkmoor as a coward, much to his intense fury, and Darkmoor was then suddenly attacked by an enraged Buckbeak, in order to protect Marcus. Darkmoor and the other Death Eaters managed to get past the gates and disapparate away from Hogwarts.

Boulderheart and Marcus extinguishing Boulderheart's burning hut

Marcus and Boulderheart then managed to extinguish his burning hut successfully. They extinguished the flames together by casting the Water-Making Spell. Marcus then informed Boulderheart of Pendragon's death, much to Boulderheart's disbelief and constant denials. However, his death was confirmed when Boulderheart and Marcus returned to the castle. Once at the castle they (along with many students and teachers) came across Pendragon's dead body lying on the ground. Pendragon's body was broken and his glasses were askew, something that Marcus quickly rectified.

Marcus kneeling next to the body of the recently deceased Alaric Pendragon

Marcus picked up Slytherin's Locket, which had fallen from Pendragon's pocket. Marcus then examined the locket and discovered it was a fake. The real locket belonged to an individual called R.A.B. In the locket was a piece of paper with a note that read "To the Dark Lord — I know I will be dead long before you read this, but I want you to know that it was I who discovered your secret. I have stolen the real Horcrux and intend to destroy it as soon as I can. I face death in the hope that when you meet your match, you will be mortal once more. — R.A.B."[14] Marcus's eye burned with tears as Fang howled, regarding the utter tragedy that had just befallen.

Because of their use of the Felix Felicis, the members of Pendragon's Army who fought in the battle were unharmed. Alexander Redwood, however, was left mildly scarred by Fenrir Greyback's attack. Because the werewolf was not fully transformed when he brutalised Alexander, his victim only retained mild-lycanthropic symptoms, such as a taste for very rare steaks. His fiancée Celeste Fontaine reaffirmed her great love and desire to marry him nonetheless, earning the respect of her future in-laws and prompting Isadora Starshadow to declare her own secret great love for Lucian Moonweaver.

Pendragon's loyal pet Phoenix Fawkes then sung his lament to the assembled mourners on the grounds, which touched all with its beauty.[13] Due to the sudden tragedy, all lessons and classes were suspended, and exams were postponed.[15]

The battle and Alaric Pendragon's death had a great impact on the entire Wizarding World. The only wizard Mortis had ever feared and who was powerful and skilled enough to surpass him, besides Marcus Thornfield, was now gone. Furthermore, the battle proved that there was now no safe place from the Death Eaters, as they had successfully and easily breached and entered the historic school, much less that they were allowed entrance by a student, and that a staff member allegedly betrayed their trust.

The funeral of Alaric Pendragon

Hogwarts staff members were not even sure whether the school would remain open from that point on, and the decision was quickly made to lay Alaric Pendragon to rest on the grounds of Hogwarts, an honour no other headmaster or headmistress of the school ever had. Some parents, such as the father of Zacharias Smith, and the parents of the Patil twins Padma and Parvati arrived to get their children out of the castle almost instantly. Others, such as Seamus Finnigan's mother, stayed in Hogsmeade and waited until after Pendragon's funeral to collect their children.[15]

Marcus, who was beyond devastated by his mentor's death, decided to forgo his last year at Hogwarts in order to search for Lord Mortis's Horcruxes, per Pendragon's instructions. Tristan and Elena committed themselves to helping him. He also broke up with Victoria Redwood, despite being with her was one of the happiest things to ever happen to him.[15]

Caspian, who was responsible for bringing in Death Eaters to the school, was forced to flee. Marcus felt some pity for him, knowing that Mortis was threatening his family. His sudden departure also led Vincent Crabbe and Gregory Goyle looking oddly lonely despite the two being hulking boys.[15] Regardless of his failure to actually kill the headmaster, Caspian could not remain at the school as his actions were considered to be criminal, and this made him realise that he went deeper than he desired towards the Dark Arts, forever changing his personality and values.

Marcus discovering that Pendragon's death had been according to his own plan

Marcus would later discover, however, that Pendragon's death had been according to his own plan, as secretly devised between him and Cassius Darkmoor, who truly was loyal to the Order of the Phoenix out of lifelong love for Lillian Thornfield, Marcus's own mother. Pendragon was already dying, long before Mortis conspired to try to have him killed, and simply wanted Darkmoor to give him a more merciful end than he would have received at the hands of Death Eaters, as well as to avoid making Caspian Silverwood into a murderer and damaging his soul.

Darkmoor fulfilled his wish, and although the Order thought him to be a traitor and a coward, Darkmoor was covertly and secretly assisting Marcus and his friends and allies, eventually becoming the new Headmaster of Hogwarts after Mortis seized control of the Ministry of Magic the following year to come. Darkmoor worked fully undercover to secretly protect the students of Hogwarts, as well as deeply contributing to the defeat of Lord Mortis.
