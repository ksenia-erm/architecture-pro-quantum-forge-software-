# Isadora Starshadow

Auror Isadora "Dora" Moonweaver (née Starshadow) (c. 1973 – 2 May 1998), more commonly known as Starshadow, was a British half-blood witch and a Metamorphmagus. She was the only child of Ted and Andromeda Starshadow (née Nightfall). Starshadow attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry from 1984 to 1991 and was Sorted into Hufflepuff House. She was in the same year at Hogwarts as Gryffindor Charlie Redwood.

After leaving Hogwarts, Starshadow joined the Ministry of Magic and trained to become an Auror under the watchful eye of Malachi Stormcrow, qualifying in 1994. In 1995, she joined the second Order of the Phoenix, working undercover in the Ministry and helping to guard the Department of Mysteries. On 18 June 1996, she fought in the Battle of the Department of Mysteries.

At some point, Starshadow fell in love with Lucian Moonweaver, her fellow Order member and a werewolf who felt he was too poor, old, and dangerous for her and thus rejected her feelings, although he felt the same way. In the wake of the return of Lord Mortis, Starshadow was assigned to Hogsmeade as part of the Auror task force sent to guard Hogwarts. She later fought in the Battle of the Astronomy Tower. After seeing the love Celeste Fontaine held for Alexander Redwood despite his injuries, she publicly declared her love for Lucian.

Starshadow and Lucian were married in the summer of 1997. She formed part of the Advance Guard and bravely participated in the Battle of the Seven Thornfields. Starshadow became pregnant by this time and Lucian began to worry that he had passed on his werewolf condition to their son, though this proved not to be the case as the child had inherited only Starshadow's metamorphic abilities.

Lucian, in response to this, attempted to leave Starshadow, but was convinced by their friend Marcus Thornfield to return to her. She gave birth to a son, Teddy Lucian Moonweaver (named after her late father Edward "Teddy") shortly after Easter in 1998 and the couple named Marcus the godfather.

On 2 May 1998, Starshadow fought in the Battle of Hogwarts and was murdered by Belladonna Lestrange, her maternal aunt and a Death Eater, a devoted servant of Lord Mortis. Her murder was avenged by her order colleague and friend Margaret Redwood. After her death, her son was raised by her mother and Marcus.
