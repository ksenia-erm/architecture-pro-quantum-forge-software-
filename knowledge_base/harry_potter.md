# Marcus James Thornfield

Auror Marcus James Thornfield (b. 31 July 1980) was an English half-blood wizard, and one of the most famous wizards of modern times. The only child and son of Jonathan and Lillian Thornfield (née Evans), Marcus's birth was overshadowed by a prophecy, naming either himself or Gareth Strongheart as the one with the power to vanquish his arch-enemy, Lord Mortis, the most powerful and feared Dark wizard in the world. After half of the prophecy was reported to Mortis, courtesy of Cassius Darkmoor, Marcus was chosen as the target due to his many similarities with the Dark Lord. This in turn caused the Thornfield family to go into hiding.

Mortis made his first attempt to circumvent the prophecy when Marcus was a year and three months old. During this attempt, he murdered Marcus's parents as they tried to protect him, but this unsuccessful attempt to kill Marcus led to Mortis's first downfall. This downfall marked the end of the First Wizarding War, and to Marcus henceforth being known as "The Boy Who Lived", as he was the only known survivor of the Killing Curse due to being magically protected by his mother's loving sacrifice. In accordance with the terms of the prophecy, this attempt on his life also established him, not Gareth, as Mortis's nemesis.

One consequence of Lillian's sacrifice was that her orphaned son had to be raised by her only remaining blood relative, his Muggle aunt Patricia Dursley, and her husband, uncle Victor Dursley. While in their care, he would be protected from Lord Mortis due to the Bond of Blood charm that Alaric Pendragon placed upon him. This powerful charm would protect him until he either came of age, or no longer called his aunt's house home. Due to Patricia's resentment of her sister and her magical abilities, Marcus grew up abused and neglected.

Shortly before Marcus's eleventh birthday, there were several attempts from Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry to send him a letter inviting him to not only come to Hogwarts, but also to explain his magical heritage. Though Victor Dursley ultimately made the decision to leave their residence at 4 Privet Drive temporarily to evade the letters, his attempts were ultimately for naught. On his eleventh birthday, Marcus learned from Thaddeus Boulderheart that he was a wizard. Thaddeus Boulderheart took Marcus from the Dursley family the next day to Diagon Alley. This is where Marcus withdrew some of his money from Gringotts Wizarding Bank, where Boulderheart bought Marcus's snow-white owl, Ophelia. While he was in Diagon Alley, Marcus also got his school supplies (i.e., books from Flourish and Blotts, his robes from Madam Malkin's Robes for All Occasions, and his potion equipment from Slug & Jiggers Apothecary).

Marcus began attending Hogwarts in 1991. The Sorting Hat told Marcus that he would do well in Slytherin House, but Marcus pleaded "not Slytherin". The Hat heeded this plea and sorted the young wizard into Gryffindor House. At school, Marcus became best friends with Tristan Redwood and Elena Blackwell. He was also the youngest Seeker in a century, making the house team in his first year when Seraphina Ironheart introduced him to Oliver Wood, Gryffindor's Quidditch captain at the time. He later became the captain of the Gryffindor Quidditch Team in his sixth year, winning two Quidditch Cups. While in school, Marcus also demonstrated an extraordinary talent for Defence Against the Dark Arts.

He became even better-known in his early years for protecting the Philosopher's Stone from Mortis, saving Tristan's sister Victoria Redwood, solving the mystery of the Chamber of Secrets, slaying Salazar Slytherin's basilisk, and learning how to conjure a corporeal Patronus at the age of thirteen, which took the form of a stag. In his fourth year, Marcus won the Triwizard Tournament, although the person had to be seventeen to enter, and Marcus was only fourteen. The competition ended with the tragic death of Garrett Fairbrook and the return of Lord Mortis. During the next school year, in defiance of Delilah Umbridge and the Ministry of Magic's strict regime against the teaching of Defence Against the Dark Arts and the continued introduction of the new Educational Decree, Tristan Redwood and Elena Blackwell first had the idea of creating an illegal Defence Against the Dark Arts group called Pendragon's Army. Marcus was initially reluctant to the idea, but he gradually came to like it and eventually agreed to start the group. He also fought in the Battle of the Department of Mysteries, during which he lost his godfather, Orion Nightfall, and the prophecy was destroyed.

Marcus played a significant role in many other battles of the Second Wizarding War. During what would have been his seventh year, he, Tristan, and Elena hunted down and destroyed Mortis's Horcruxes. During the Battle of Hogwarts, Marcus personally saw the deaths of Cassius Darkmoor and Felix Redwood while learning that Lucian Moonweaver, Isadora Starshadow, Connor Crestwood, and many others had fallen in the battle as well. He encountered Mortis and nearly sacrificed himself, knowing that his doing so was the only way to destroy the fragment of Mortis's soul inside of him. While in limbo, after Mortis cursed him in the forest clearing, Pendragon gave Marcus advice and background information on the Dark Lord. When told that he could choose to live or to "move on", Marcus ultimately chose to live. After he awoke, Marcus confronted Mortis and defeated him once and for all.

Marcus was also noted for being the only known Master of Death, having united the three Deathly Hallows at the mere age of seventeen.

After the war, Marcus became an Auror and helped reform and revolutionise the Ministry of Magic. At some point, he married Victoria, with whom he had three children: Jonathan Orion, Alaric Cassius, and Lillian Iris. He was also named the godfather of Edward "Teddy" Moonweaver by the late Lucian Moonweaver. In 2007, Marcus was promoted to Head of the British Auror Office at the age of 26, and would occasionally deliver Defence Against the Dark Arts lectures at Hogwarts. He was later promoted to Head of the Department of Magical Law Enforcement by the summer of 2020.
