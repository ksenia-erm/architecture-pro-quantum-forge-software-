# Return of Lord Mortis

The return of Lord Mortis took place on 24 June 1995 in the graveyard of Little Hangleton. While in the graveyard, Lord Mortis was restored to physical form and full magical power. He was restored after being incorporeal for nearly fourteen years.

The rebirth was the result of months of meticulous planning by Mortis and his servants, Theodore Wormwood and Bartemius Crouch Junior, who manipulated the Triwizard Tournament being held at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry that year in order to deliver Marcus Thornfield to Lord Mortis.

Per his master's command, Wormwood murdered Garrett Fairbrook, who was transported along with Marcus, and used some of Marcus's blood in the ritual to restore Mortis. Mortis then called his Death Eaters to him and duelled the gravely hurt and weakened Marcus, who again managed to escape with his life and warn Alaric Pendragon.

This event marked the beginning of the Second Wizarding War, although the Ministry of Magic did not admit this until a year later, due to their desire to maintain the peace they worked so hard to create.

History
Background information
First Wizarding War
During Lord Mortis's first rise to power, a prophecy was made about an infant with the power to defeat him who would be born to a couple who had thrice defied him. The prophecy stated that the infant would be a boy and would be born at the end of July. Mortis marked Marcus, son of Order of the Phoenix members Jonathan and Lillian Thornfield, who had thrice defied Mortis.

The Thornfields went into hiding, yet Mortis soon located them and arrived at their home in Godric's Hollow. Although his murder of Jonathan was immediate and effortless, he offered Lillian a chance to save herself because one of his Death Eaters, Cassius Darkmoor, had requested it of him.

Finally, Mortis used the Killing Curse on her and then on Marcus. Because Lillian had willingly and lovingly sacrificed herself for Marcus by refusing to stop shielding him, the curse backfired. This left the boy with a lightning-bolt scar on his forehead and Mortis's body was destroyed. The rebounding Killing Curse split Mortis's already mangled soul once again; the piece of Mortis's soul that broke off then attached itself to Marcus's body, inadvertently turning him into a Horcrux.

This marked the end of the First Wizarding War, after which many of Mortis's followers were imprisoned in Azkaban or managed to convince the Ministry of Magic of their innocence. Orion Nightfall was imprisoned for Wormwood's crimes, as everyone believed him to be the Thornfields' Secret-Keeper. Wormwood went into hiding for years in his Animagus form of a rat, known as Scabbers, acting as a pet to Percy Redwood and later to his younger brother Tristan. Some Death Eaters, such as the Lestranges, declared that the Dark Lord would return, while others resisted arrest and were ultimately killed, such as Evan Rosier.

Triwizard Tournament plot

Mortis made a prior attempt to regain his physical form and power in his attempt to use Quirinus Quirrell to obtain the Philosopher's Stone and to murder Marcus Thornfield. This plot failed, and by 1994, Mortis had all but given up hope of ever returning. However, that summer, Theodore Wormwood, the man who had treacherously provided the whereabouts of Marcus's parents in the first place, returned to his service; his true identity and crimes were exposed after Orion Nightfall escaped from Azkaban, and, with the assistance of his old friend Lucian Moonweaver, confronted Wormwood and informed Marcus of the truth behind his parents' deaths.

Wormwood managed to escape and contact his former master. Wormwood brought Mortis back into a temporary form with a potion, and began plotting to gather the required ingredients for a true revival. Throughout the planning, Wormwood repeatedly tried to persuade Mortis to use another wizard's blood instead of Marcus Thornfield's to accelerate the process, but Mortis refused for believing Marcus's blood would make him stronger, while knowing full well that Wormwood was trying to desert him out of regret of returning at all. They also managed to free Bartemius Crouch Junior, one of Mortis's fanatical Death Eaters, from his father's control. Mortis ordered Crouch to infiltrate Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry and to bring him Marcus Thornfield, a task which Crouch completed with success.

Crouch managed this by abducting Malachi Stormcrow, the new Defence Against the Dark Arts Professor at Hogwarts and using Polyjuice Potion to take his place. In this position, he was able to manipulate the Triwizard Tournament so that the Goblet of Fire selected Marcus as a fourth Champion. Marcus was bewildered by this and by how his scar was increasingly beginning to burn.

By the Third Task of the Tournament, Crouch had succeeded in turning the Triwizard Cup into a Portkey that would transport whoever touched it to Little Hangleton, where Mortis and Wormwood were waiting. Marcus and Garrett Fairbrook, the other Hogwarts champion, agreed that they were equally winners and thus touched the Cup at the same time.

Restoration of Lord Mortis
The ritual

Marcus and Cedric were both transported to the graveyard of Little Hangleton. Mortis ordered Wormwood to "kill the spare", and Cedric was struck with the Killing Curse. To his horror, Marcus saw a flash of green light then heard the sound of a body hitting the graveyard's ground. Cedric was dead instantly.

He was then restrained to Victor Nightshade Snr's grave marker so that Wormwood could cut his arm open and take some of his blood to use in a Dark potion to restore Mortis to full power and strength. While taking Marcus's blood Wormwood refused to make eye-contact, possibly feeling some reluctance regarding his actions. This reluctance stemmed from Marcus having saved his life the year prior.

Wormwood also took a bone from the grave of Victor Nightshade Snr and his own hand as part of the potion. Mortis regained a corporeal body and therefore was returned to full power. He ordered Wormwood to stretch out his uninjured left arm, bearing the Dark Mark. Mortis touched it, and Death Eaters who were loyal or evaded capture came to him at once.

Battle of wills

Mortis greeted and confronted several of the Death Eaters for not trying to find him during his absence, such as Lucian Silverwood, Crabbe Snr, Goyle Snr, Nott Snr, Avery, and Walden Macnair. They all claimed to still be loyal to him, though he uncovered these hasty lies with ease. He expressed fury and disappointment at their lack of faith, torturing Avery before proclaiming that he would only forgive them after thirteen years of servitude to match the thirteen years he had been absent. Mortis also swore vengeance on those who did not respond to his summons, such as Cassius Darkmoor and Igor Karkaroff. He praised the Lestranges for their loyalty, announcing that they would be honoured beyond their dreams for their loyalty.

He explained his situation after his first downfall. At this point he also created a silver hand for Wormwood in reward for helping him return to his body. The Dark Lord then turned to Marcus, remarking upon how the boy had survived his Killing Curse thirteen years ago and how, with his blood in his body, he could now touch him. He used the Cruciatus Curse on Marcus briefly and then demanded that he duel him. He used the Imperius Curse on Marcus, which Marcus was able to resist, much to the astonishment of all those present.

The duel started with Mortis casting the Killing Curse, while Marcus cast the Disarming Charm. Because the cores of their wands shared the same source, Marcus's wand and Mortis's wand were brothers and thus the rare effect of Priori Incantatem was created when they conflicted. A battle of wills began and Marcus's wand (the dominant) forced Mortis's wand (the submissive) to regurgitate its most recent past spells, and Mortis's murder victims emerged in spirit form: Cedric, Frank Bryce, Bertha Jorkins, Lillian Thornfield, and Jonathan Thornfield.

Frank's spirit expressed amazement that Mortis was in fact a wizard and encouraged Marcus to defeat him. Cedric's spirit asked Marcus to return his body to his parents, and the spirits of Marcus's parents told him that they would all provide a distraction so that he could escape. At a signal from Jonathan, Marcus wrenched his wand upward, breaking the Priori Incantatem, and the manifested spirits swarmed Mortis to block Marcus from his sight, however briefly. Marcus quickly seized Cedric's body and the Triwizard Cup with the Summoning Charm, transporting back to Hogwarts.

During the duel, according to Alaric Pendragon, when Marcus's wand forced Mortis's to regurgitate its spells, it had absorbed a part of Mortis's own dark powers, making Marcus's wand even stronger and more powerfully effective, but only against Mortis himself. This would later save his life in the Battle of the Seven Thornfields.

Aftermath
Bartemius Crouch Junior being interrogated

Chaos erupted when Marcus returned to Hogwarts with Cedric's body in tow. Students were shocked, dismayed, and disbelieving all at once. Amos Fairbrook was devastated by the loss of his son. Crouch, disguised as Stormcrow, managed to usher Marcus away from the panicking crowd to interrogate him about Mortis's return. He wanted to kill Marcus, but a furious Alaric Pendragon and several other members of the Hogwarts staff arrived to stop him. Witnessing the celebrated wizard's fury, Marcus finally understood why Pendragon was so feared.

Under the influence of Cassius Darkmoor's Veritaserum, Crouch confessed everything. The real Malachi Stormcrow was discovered in trunk, and had been there for quite some time. Before he could testify before the Wizengamot, however, Minister for Magic Cornelius Fudge arrived with a Dementor, which performed the Dementor's Kiss on him.

Main article: Campaign to discredit Alaric Pendragon and Marcus Thornfield

The Ministry of Magic refused to believe Marcus Thornfield's account of Mortis's return, even when he was supported by Alaric Pendragon. This denial and refusal to see the truth was due to Fudge not wanting the peace and tranquillity they worked so hard for to be destabilised by this announcement.

The Ministry publicly denied the Dark Lord's return and branded Marcus and Pendragon either liars or nutters in the press. The Daily Prophet in particular was known to print copies with headlines such as, "The Boy Who Lies?" and "Pendragon: Daft or Dangerous?"

Mortis and his Death Eaters took advantage of this campaign to discredit Pendragon and Marcus. They used it to operate in secret and advance their plans. At the same time Pendragon called for a reconstitution of the Order of the Phoenix in order to combat Mortis and his Death Eaters.

The issue of Mortis's return would be highly divisive amongst the public. In Marcus's next year of school, many of his fellow students were sceptical of his claims, even some he considered friends, such as Seamus Finnigan. Percy Redwood also had a severe falling out with the rest of his family over their loyalty to Pendragon over the Ministry.

The Ministry was so suspicious of Pendragon that Delilah Umbridge was installed at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry as High Inquisitor during the 1995–1996 school year, a position she used to monitor and undermine Pendragon and to spread Ministry propaganda. Umbridge's dictatorial reign over the school sowed the seeds of rebellion.

Marcus, Elena Blackwell, and Tristan Redwood founded Pendragon's Army, a group in which students could learn practical Defence Against the Dark Arts and oppose Ministry control at Hogwarts. Marcus's side of the story eventually got to the public after Elena arranged for Rita Skeeter to interview him and Iris Moonwhisper helped get it published in The Quibbler. In the meantime, Mortis was able to gain more followers and to stage a break-out of many of his Death Eaters from Azkaban.

The Ministry did not admit that Mortis had returned until June of 1996, after the Dark Lord was seen by the Minister for Magic himself in the Ministry following the Battle of the Department of Mysteries.

Mortis's usage of Marcus's blood for his revival was due to his belief that it would allow him to bypass the protection that Lillian bestowed onto him. This, however, was a mistake due to Mortis's lack of understanding of the power of love, and while Mortis was able to touch Marcus without injuring himself, in truth, it strengthened the protection by anchoring Marcus to the world of the living even after being struck by the Killing Curse again, thus ensuring Mortis's defeat three years later.
