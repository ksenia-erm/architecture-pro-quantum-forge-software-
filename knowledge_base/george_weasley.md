# Gideon Redwood

Gideon Redwood (b. 1 April 1978) was an English pure-blood wizard, the fifth son and the less dominant among the twins of Richard Redwood and Margaret Redwood (née Prewett), younger brother of Alexander, Charlie and Percy, younger twin brother and best friend of the late Felix Redwood, and older brother to Tristan and Victoria. Gideon's first few years were marked by the height of the First Wizarding War and Lord Mortis's first fall.

He attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry from 1989 to 1996 with Felix, and was Sorted into Gryffindor House. He and Felix were popular students, joining the Circle of Khanna in their first year, becoming Beaters for the Gryffindor Quidditch team in their second year and members of Pendragon's Army in their unfinished seventh year.

A born prankster and inventor, Gideon left Hogwarts prior to graduation to become a successful entrepreneur with Felix, and opened the joke shop Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes at 93 Diagon Alley. He was a member of the Order of the Phoenix and fought in the Battle of the Seven Thornfields and the Battle of Hogwarts; in the latter, Felix died, leaving Gideon devastated.

Sometime after the end of the Second Wizarding War, Gideon married Angelina Johnson, with whom he had a son named Felix in honour of his late twin, and a daughter named Roxanne. He and his brother Tristan also turned Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes into a huge money spinner.
