# Skirmish in the Chamber of Secrets

This skirmish was a conflict that took place on 29 May 1993 in the Chamber of Secrets, wherein Marcus Thornfield slew Salazar Slytherin's Basilisk and destroyed Victor Nightshade's Diary.

It was precipitated when one of Lord Mortis's Horcruxes, Victor Nightshade's Diary, possessed first year Victoria Redwood and forced her to open the Chamber of Secrets and write frightening messages on the walls. As the 1992–1993 school year came to an end Riddle forced her into the Chamber itself.

Marcus Thornfield, Tristan Redwood, and Gilderoy Lockhart came to the rescue. Tristan and Lockhart were stuck behind an avalanche of stone, so the battle was fought mostly by Marcus. Marcus managed to defeat both the Basilisk and the memory of Victor Nightshade, thus saving Victoria's life. He also unknowingly eliminated one of Mortis's Horcruxes.

History
Background information

On 31 December 1926, Victor Nightshade was born to the pure-blood witch Merope Gaunt in a London orphanage. He was an odd boy and discovered in his eleventh year that he was a wizard. Believing death to be the realm of the weak, he initially believed his mother could not have been magical, given her death, and the young Victor focused on finding his father among the records at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

However, his search was fruitless, as Victor Nightshade Snr had never set foot in the school. After discovering this, he turned his attention to his mother's family and found the Gaunt line, direct descendants of Salazar Slytherin himself leaving near Little Hangleton.

Riddle took some time to research his famous ancestor and in his fifth year, he found the fabled Chamber of Secrets. Having come to believe in fanatical pure-blood supremacy, Riddle planned to use the "terror within" the chamber to rid the school of those "unworthy" to learn magic; that is, Muggle-born wizards and witches. Throughout the 1942-1943 school year, a series of Muggle-born students were petrified.

Finally, late in the school year, Myrtle Warren was killed, and the Headmaster threatened to close the school unless the culprit was apprehended. Not wanting to return to the Muggle orphanage from which he came, Riddle decided that he needed a patsy. He chose third year Gryffindor Thaddeus Boulderheart, who had a reputation already and took the information to Professor Dippet, the Headmaster.

Boulderheart was expelled but allowed to remain as Gamekeeper, because the Transfiguration professor, Alaric Pendragon, did not believe him to be guilty. Riddle used the girl's death to create his first Horcrux, Victor Nightshade's Diary, but he did not want his work forgotten, so later in his life, he planned with Lucian Silverwood that the diary makes its way back to Hogwarts, where it could finish Salazar Slytherin's work.

After Mortis's first defeat

Therefore, he planted the diary on Victoria Redwood in Flourish and Blotts in Diagon Alley, the daughter of the Act's chief proponent and his enemy, Richard Redwood. He hoped that if the authorities discovered Richard Redwood's daughter attacking Muggle-borns, the Act would die with Richard Redwood's career. Silverwood did not care if Victoria or others were harmed in the process.

When Victoria discovered the diary among her possessions, she began to use it, and slowly, the piece of Victor Nightshade's soul inside began to exert an influence over the young girl. By Hallowe'en, it could possess her entirely, and that night, it forced her to open the Chamber of Secrets for the first time in fifty years and to attack Mrs Norris. Panic set in at Hogwarts as more students were attacked, including Connor Crestwood and Justin Finch-Fletchley. In December, when Marcus Thornfield revealed that he was a Parselmouth in front of the entire Duelling Club, many students began to believe that he was the Heir of Slytherin. After the double attack on Justin Finch-Fletchley and Nearly-Headless Nick, the rash of attacks suddenly stopped.

Shortly before Valentine's Day, Marcus found an old diary abandoned in Myrtle Warren's bathroom. While he found nothing in the diary, he was drawn to it. Late in the evening, he wrote in the diary and was shocked to see it write back. When asked about the Chamber of Secrets, the diary showed Marcus Victor Nightshade's capture of Boulderheart. Not willing to believe that Boulderheart was the culprit, Marcus decided to ask him about it if another attack occurred.

Meanwhile, Victoria stole the diary back from Marcus so it would not divulge her secrets. On a Saturday, early in May, Elena Blackwell and Penelope Clearwater were attacked, and Marcus and Tristan felt that it was time to question Boulderheart. Unfortunately, Boulderheart was arrested by Minister for Magic Cornelius Fudge, and Professor Pendragon was suspended that night, but not before Boulderheart gave them a cryptic clue about spiders.


Following Boulderheart's clue, they ventured into the Forbidden Forest with Boulderheart's dog Fang, and encountered Aragog, an Acromantula and Boulderheart's pet, who informed them that he was not the monster who attacked students, with them only narrowing escaping his carnivorous colony with the Flying Ford Anglia. After this event, they both learned that Boulderheart was innocent and that the girl who died was killed in a bathroom. Putting these clues together with Elena's clue about the basilisk, Marcus was able to intuit that the entrance to the Chamber of Secrets was in the second-floor girls' lavatory.

Discovering the Chamber

On 29 May, when the petrified students were to be cured and it appeared that life at Hogwarts was going to return to normal, the diary possessed Victoria again, forcing her to go down to the Chamber of Secrets and await her death. Before she went into the Chamber for the final time, the diary coerced her into scrawling "Her skeleton will lie in the Chamber forever" on the wall. This alerted the faculty to her disappearance.

They appointed Gilderoy Lockhart to save her, as Lockhart had said the previous night at dinner he knew where the entrance to the Chamber was, mainly to get him out of the way, while they prepared for the worst. Lockhart, a charlatan, tried to sneak out of the school, but was stopped by Marcus Thornfield and Tristan Redwood, who intended to save Victoria Redwood.

At wand point, Marcus and Tristan forced Lockhart to take them to the bathroom. Once there, Marcus asked Myrtle about her death in the hope that she would confirm his hypothesis. She claimed that she heard a boy's voice speaking a strange language and then saw a pair of great yellow eyes.

When he further inspected the lavatory, Marcus found a broken tap with a snake engraved in the handle. He spoke to the engraving in Parseltongue, and the entrance to the Chamber opened, the entrance behind the sink was a pipe big enough for a man to crawl through. Ready to take the plunge, Marcus and Tristan forced Lockhart into the opening and followed.

Entering the Chamber

Upon entering a series of maze-like dungeons, Marcus, Tristan, and Lockhart set out in search of Victoria. They found nothing until they came across a great snake skin, upon seeing which Lockhart feigned a collapse. Tristan tried to force him to his feet, but Lockhart grabbed Tristan's wand and told the boys that he was going to wipe their memories and escape.

However, his plan failed, as the Memory Charm backfired, causing the ceiling to cave in. Tristan and an amnesiac Lockhart were stuck behind the wall of rocks, and Marcus had to venture into the Chamber by himself. As Marcus turned to head deeper in the dungeon, Tristan told Marcus he would try moving some of the rock while he waited.

Marcus next reached a set of doors engraved with serpents, and when he spoke in Parseltongue, they opened, allowing him into the Chamber properly. Reaching the end of the Chamber, Marcus discovered Victoria collapsed at the foot of a statue of Salazar Slytherin.

Meeting Victor Nightshade

As Marcus tried to wake her, Victor Nightshade appeared behind him telling Marcus that she would not wake. Marcus tried to tell Victor that they were in great danger, but Victor just scoffed. This confused Marcus, as he had seen Riddle's capture of Boulderheart and believed that he too wanted to stop the attacks. Then Riddle began asking Marcus how he survived Mortis's attack on him when he was a baby, which worried and confused Marcus more.

He then showed Marcus that his name, Victor Marvolo Nightshade, could be rearranged into I am Lord Mortis. After Marcus realised the truth, Riddle continued to question him. They argued over the merits of Mortis's magical experimentation, and Marcus told Riddle that Pendragon was the greatest sorcerer in the world. His loyalty summoned Fawkes, Pendragon's phoenix. Fawkes brought the old school Sorting Hat. When satisfactorily answered, Riddle used Parseltongue to order Salazar Slytherin's Basilisk to attack Marcus. "Speak to me, Slytherin, greatest of the Hogwarts Four".

Battling the Basilisk

Pendragon's phoenix, Fawkes, then came to the rescue. He was able to blind the Basilisk, which allowed Marcus to fight her without the fear of being killed instantly by her gaze. While Marcus ran with his eyes covered, Fawkes dug his claws into the basilisk's eyes, blinding it and allowing Marcus to look at the monster. Unfortunately, the basilisk had an acute sense of hearing and smell. It continued its attack on Marcus, but Marcus dodged each time it lunged. Unsure of what to do, Marcus suddenly saw Godric Gryffindor's Sword appear from the hat.

Marcus lured the serpent to the top of the Statue of Slytherin, where he slashed at the evil beast fiercely. The Basilisk lunged at him, and knocked Marcus over, but just as she lunged again to kill him, Marcus grabbed the sword and impaled the Basilisk through the roof of her mouth, mortally wounding her. Unfortunately, one of her fangs pierced Marcus in his sword arm as a result of the struggle, and splintered off as the Basilisk pulled away and flailed around in agony before she ultimately lost her ability to live and her massive weight finally brought her crashing down to the floor.

Marcus yanked the fang out, but knew it was too late: he had been wounded with her venom, and he felt the poison spreading. Marcus was only saved by Fawkes's tears, and subsequently used the fang (on which his arm had been impaled) to destroy Riddle's diary, which he later discovered was one of Mortis's seven Horcruxes.

Destroying Victor Nightshade's Diary
As Marcus lay dying with the poison running through his veins, Riddle taunted him, saying that "he'll be reunited with his dear Mudblood mother soon. Marcus grabbed the fang and plunged it into the pages and front cover of the diary, destroying Riddle beyond magical repair, saving Victoria's life and unknowingly eliminating one of Lord Mortis's seven Horcruxes.

The Horcrux essentially died with ink pouring from its pages like blood from an open wound and addiction with Riddle's screams. The diary was rendered useless and unable to function. Fawkes then saved Marcus again by using his tears to heal Marcus's wound poisoned by the basilisk venom.

Aftermath

When Marcus, Tristan, Victoria, and Lockhart emerged from the Chamber, they headed to the office of Seraphina Ironheart. Pendragon was in there when they arrived, as were Victoria's parents, Richard and Margaret Redwood. Marcus explained all that had happened, but tread lightly around Victoria's involvement. Pendragon came to his rescue, by asking how Mortis managed to enchant Victoria. Marcus explained all about the Diary. Victoria was not punished, and Marcus and Tristan both received Special Awards for Services to the School and 200 points each for Gryffindor. Marcus exposed Lockhart as a fraud, and he was sent to St Mungo's Hospital for Magical Maladies and Injuries.

After slaying the Basilisk, the sword was impregnated with Basilisk venom; Basilisk venom was one of the rare ways to destroy a Horcrux, as its only cure was also incredibly rare: phoenix tears. Since the sword was in his possession, Alaric Pendragon used it to destroy Marvolo Gaunt's Ring, and, after his death, he left it to Marcus Thornfield. Tristan Redwood used it to destroy the locket horcrux, and Gareth Strongheart used it to decapitate Nagini.
