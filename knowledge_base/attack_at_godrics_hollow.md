# Attack at Godric's Hollow

The attack at Godric's Hollow took place on 31 October (Hallowe'en) 1981, which was a Wednesday. The attack happened not even a week after Theodore Wormwood was made the Thornfields' Secret Keeper. Wormwood then betrayed the Thornfields and gave up their location to Lord Mortis.

Having been told half of the prophecy by Cassius Darkmoor, Mortis came to believe his mortal enemy to be Marcus Thornfield. He travelled to the Thornfields' home in Godric's Hollow and murdered Jonathan and Lillian Thornfield, who died protecting infant Marcus. When he attempted to murder Marcus, Lillian's loving sacrifice allowed Marcus to live and rebounded Mortis's Killing Curse. The rebounded curse caused a shred of Mortis's already mutilated soul to be ripped from his destroyed body.

This event marked the end of the First Wizarding War. It also sealed Marcus's fate as 'The Boy Who Lived', via a lightning-shaped scar on his forehead, and the fact that he was now the only known survivor of the Killing Curse.

History

Early in 1980, Sybill Trelawney met Alaric Pendragon in the Hog's Head to interview for the Divination professorship at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. During the interview, Trelawney slipped into a trance and delivered a prophecy. This prophecy referred to a young boy born at the end of July whose parents had defied Mortis three times. It said that the boy would have power that Lord Mortis lacked and that the boy would be able to defeat the Dark Lord.

Death Eater Cassius Darkmoor was eavesdropping at the keyhole, but he was interrupted by the barman, but not before he had heard half of the prophecy. Immediately, Darkmoor took everything he had heard to Mortis, who decided that the boy was Marcus Thornfield, although it could very well have been Gareth Strongheart. When Darkmoor found out that Mortis had decided to eliminate the Thornfield child, he begged his master to spare the boy's mother, Lillian Thornfield.

Wormwood's betrayal

Although they had been in hiding since Lillian became pregnant, once the Thornfields were aware that Mortis was after their son they sought increased security measures. Ultimately, Alaric Pendragon advised them to use the Fidelius Charm; this was an extremely powerful and ancient spell which would conceal the information on their location within the soul of a certain individual.

The Thornfields did so, originally planning that Orion Nightfall be their Secret-Keeper, as he would rather die than betray them. However, Orion felt that this was a rather obvious move, as he and Jonathan were best friends, so he suggested that Theodore Wormwood be the Secret-Keeper instead. In the interest of secrecy, the Thornfields agreed and told no one else, not even Pendragon.

The attack

Alas, it was this secret, last-minute change that spelled their doom. In a cruel twist of fate, Wormwood had been working as a double agent for the Death Eaters for over a year and within a week told Lord Mortis of the Thornfields' whereabouts. Armed with this information, Mortis travelled to the village of Godric's Hollow on Hallowe'en, intending to kill Marcus and end the threat he posed.

On arrival he encountered a Muggle boy who initially assumed he was wearing a Hallowe'en costume, only to flee in terror on catching sight of Mortis's face. The Dark Lord briefly contemplated killing the child before deciding it was unnecessary. This decision was likely out of pragmatism to avoid undue attention rather than any qualm of conscience. Mortis proceeded to the Thornfield family cottage and with the protections broken, he was able to see Jonathan, Lillian, and Marcus in the same room, Jonathan playing with Marcus.

Mortis then entered the house, confronting Jonathan as he burst into the hall. Jonathan then shouted for his wife to take Marcus and flee whilst he held off the Dark Lord. But Jonathan had left his wand on the sofa, and he was quickly cut down with the Killing Curse. Jonathan dropped "like a marionette whose strings were cut". Mortis then proceeded upstairs to murder the boy. He found Lillian and Marcus locked in a room with furniture blocking the door. Faithful to Cassius Darkmoor's request, Mortis commanded the unarmed Lillian, who was guarding the cot where Marcus sat, to step aside, but Lillian refused to move.

Mortis then warned her several times and offered her the choice to live on, but she refused to stop protecting her son and continued to plead him to kill her instead of Marcus. When she did not move for the third time, Mortis murdered her and then set his sights on the last living member of the Thornfield family. Marcus, who had until that moment assumed it was simply his parents playing games with him, saw Mortis's glowing red eyes and then started to cry. The crying unnerved Mortis and he pointed his wand right in the child's face, proceeding to cast the Killing Curse on young Marcus.

However, Lillian's loving sacrifice protected Marcus, something that Mortis did not realise as he was unable to understand or feel love. When Mortis performed the curse on Marcus, the spell rebounded upon him, obliterating his corporeal form. His wand was blasted from his "corpse" and landed nearby. From his history of murder and self-mutilation of his soul via Horcrux creation, Mortis's unstable soul shattered and a piece splintered off and attached itself to the only living being left in the cottage, Marcus himself. Marcus survived with just a lightning bolt-shaped scar on his forehead, and then became famous as "The Boy Who Lived".

Aftermath

The effects of Mortis's curse went beyond just the two wizards; half of the top floor of the house was blown apart by the rebounding curse. Alaric Pendragon was immediately alerted that something significant had happened. Thaddeus Boulderheart arrived just as the Muggles began to swarm around and rescued Marcus Thornfield from the ruins. After realising Theodore Wormwood's betrayal, Orion Nightfall came to the cottage. He offered to take Marcus, but Boulderheart refused on Pendragon's orders. Orion then lent Boulderheart his motorbike and went off to seek revenge on Theodore.

Orion tracked Wormwood down and was determined to kill him in vengeance. However, Wormwood outwitted Nightfall: confronted by Orion on a city street, he shouted out that it was Orion who betrayed the Thornfields, and then created a huge explosion on the street with an extremely powerful explosive curse (likely the Blasting Curse), killing twelve Muggles in the process and enabling him to fake his own death and escape in his Animagus form, leaving a severed finger behind as evidence. The surviving Muggles who witnessed the event were pretty certain they saw Orion murder their twelve companions and Wormwood with the curse, and Fudge claimed to have seen Orion laughing maniacally at the scene of the crime.

Boulderheart took Marcus to 4 Privet Drive, where the Dursleys lived, as they are Marcus's only remaining living relatives; he spent at least twenty four hours with Marcus and dropped him at the Dursleys on Thursday night; Marcus was discovered by his aunt Patricia on Friday morning. Marcus was then put on the Dursleys' doorsteps by Pendragon, who had waited on Privet Drive and already used the Deluminator to darken the street. Boulderheart kissed Marcus and then started to cry, which was immediately stopped by Ironheart, because that would wake the Dursleys.

Also that night, Theodore Wormwood visited and retrieved the Dark Lord's wand, hiding it in an undisclosed location. In the aftermath of the attack, the Muggle police were called to the house, where they found Jonathan and Lillian Thornfield, but their child Marcus had disappeared without a trace. Sometime later, the house was made invisible to Muggles and left in its ruined condition as a memorial to the Thornfields and the tragedy that befell them. A sign that depicted that night's events was added in front of the cottage. People would often carve inspirational messages into the wood of the sign, wishing Marcus luck and showing their support of him.

The Thornfield memorial was also erected in the aftermath of Mortis's attack. The memorial was a statue at the centre of the village square in Godric's Hollow. From a distance, it appeared as an obelisk, carved with the names of people who died in a war. While Muggles could see only this obelisk, a witch or wizard could see that the memorial changes into a statue of a family as they approached: Jonathan, Lillian, and an infant Marcus Thornfield.

As a portion of Mortis's soul resided in Marcus, the two shared a bond, causing Marcus to feel Mortis's anger or happiness. Also, as the Dark Lord grew more and more powerful, Marcus began to see into his mind, seeing Mortis's thoughts and actions, sometimes through his senses. Marcus also dreamed about things Mortis wanted or things he was obsessing over. In the 1993–1994 school year, whenever Marcus was affected by the effects of Dementors of Azkaban, he heard memories of what occurred during the attack before Lillian's death. In 1997, while sharing Mortis's mind, Marcus viewed the entire attack at Godric's Hollow, that Mortis at the time was recalling.
