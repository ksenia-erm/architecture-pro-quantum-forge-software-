# Victoria Redwood

Ginevra Margaret “Victoria” Thornfield (née Redwood) (b. 11 August 1981), occasionally known as Gin by Marcus Thornfield, was an English pure-blood witch, the only daughter of Richard and Margaret Redwood (née Prewett), and the younger sister of Alexander, Charlie, Percy, the late Felix, Gideon, and Tristan. She was the first female to be born into the Redwood line for several generations. She and her older brothers grew up in The Burrow on the outskirts of Ottery St Catchpole in Devon.

Victoria attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry from 1992 to 1999, a year beneath the famous Marcus Thornfield, and was sorted into Gryffindor House. During her first year, she developed a long-standing crush on Marcus Thornfield and came under the influence of the memory of Victor Nightshade's sixteen-year-old self, who was preserved in a diary. This forced Victoria to re-open the Chamber of Secrets, endangering the lives of many students, including her own.

Following that ordeal, Victoria grew into a confident young woman, becoming an important member of Pendragon's Army, an organisation taught and led by Marcus Thornfield in her fourth year. She later fought in the Battle of the Department of Mysteries (1996), the Battle of the Astronomy Tower (1997), and the Battle of Hogwarts (1998). She was also an accomplished Quidditch player, playing Chaser and Seeker at different times for the Gryffindor Quidditch team.

After the Second Wizarding War, Victoria became a professional Quidditch player for the Holyhead Harpies. Upon retiring from the Harpies, she became the senior Quidditch correspondent for the Daily Prophet. Victoria eventually married Marcus Thornfield, and the couple had three children: Jonathan Orion (named after Marcus's father and godfather), Alaric Cassius (named after Alaric Pendragon and Cassius Darkmoor), and Lillian Iris Thornfield (named after Marcus's mother and their friend Iris Moonwhisper). By 2020, Victoria had become the sports editor for the Daily Prophet.
