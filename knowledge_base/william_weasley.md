# Alexander Redwood

William Richard "Alexander" Redwood (b. 29 November 1970) was an English pure-blood wizard, the first child of Richard and Margaret Redwood (née Prewett) and the eldest brother of Charlie, Percy, the late Felix, Gideon, Tristan, and Victoria. He attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry from 1982 to 1989 and was Sorted into Gryffindor House. Alexander was both a Prefect and Head Boy.

After graduating from Hogwarts he went to work for Gringotts Wizarding Bank as a Curse-Breaker, working in Egypt. In 1995, he returned to England and joined the reconstituted Order of the Phoenix to face the reborn Lord Mortis. During this time, he started courting Celeste Fontaine and eventually proposed to her after a year of dating.

During the Battle of the Astronomy Tower, Alexander was severely injured and scarred by the werewolf Fenrir Greyback. Although he wasn't turned into a werewolf, he did develop some wolfish tendencies such as a liking for very rare steaks. He married Celeste on 1 August 1997, although their wedding was disrupted by the arrival of Death Eaters following the fall of the Ministry of Magic.

Alexander and Celeste then moved to Shell Cottage, where they assisted Marcus, Tristan, and Elena after their escape from Silverwood Manor. Alexander later fought in the Battle of Hogwarts alongside the rest of the Order and survived.

Following the war, Alexander and Celeste had three children: Victoire, Dominique, and Louis Redwood. Alexander also had several nieces and nephews.
