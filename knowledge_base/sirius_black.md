# Orion Nightfall

Orion Nightfall III (3 November 1959 – 18 June 1996), also known as Shadowpaw or Snuffles (in his Animagus form), was an English pure-blood wizard, the eldest son of Orion and Walburga Nightfall, the older brother of Regulus Nightfall, and godfather of Marcus Thornfield. Although he was the heir of the House of Nightfall, Orion disagreed with his family's belief in blood purity and defied tradition when he was Sorted into Gryffindor House instead of Slytherin at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, which he attended from 1971 to 1978. As the rest of his family had been in Slytherin, he was the odd one out.

As Orion's relationship with his relatives deteriorated, he gained a lifelong friendship with Jonathan Thornfield and Lucian Moonweaver. He was also friends with Theodore Wormwood. The four friends, also known as the Marauders, joined the Order of the Phoenix to fight against Lord Mortis and his Death Eaters during the First Wizarding War. Orion was named the godfather of Marcus James Thornfield, the only son of Jonathan and Lillian Thornfield. When Wormwood betrayed the Thornfields to Mortis, Orion sought to get revenge on Wormwood. However, Wormwood was able to frame Orion for his betrayal of the Thornfields, the murder of twelve Muggles, and the staged murder of Wormwood before Orion could accomplish this.

Orion was sent to Azkaban and, after twelve years, became the only known person to escape the prison unassisted. He did it by transforming into his Animagus form of a massive black dog often confused with a "Grim", an omen said to cause death. Orion exposed Wormwood's treachery to his old friend Lucian and his godson Marcus. Unfortunately, Dementors attacked the group and allowed Wormwood to escape. With the Minister for Magic Cornelius Fudge wanting to have Orion kissed by the Dementors and Orion's proof of innocence gone, he was forced to go on the run.

After Lord Mortis returned in 1995, Orion rejoined the Order. He was killed by his cousin Belladonna Lestrange during the Battle of the Department of Mysteries and subsequently cleared of all charges by the Ministry of Magic, though it was too late for him to enjoy his freedom. Eventually, his death was avenged by Margaret Redwood.

He briefly appeared again to Marcus through the Resurrection Stone on 2 May 1998, along with Jonathan and Lillian Thornfield, and Lucian Moonweaver. Marcus later named his first son Jonathan Orion Thornfield after him and his father Jonathan Thornfield I.
