# Cassius Darkmoor

Professor Cassius Darkmoor (9 January 1960 – 2 May 1998) was an English half-blood wizard serving as Potions Master (1981-1996), Head of Slytherin House (1981-1997), Defence Against the Dark Arts professor (1996-1997), and Headmaster (1997-1998) of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry as well as a member of both the Order of the Phoenix and a Death Eater. His double life played an extremely important role in both of the First and Second Wizarding Wars against Mortis.

The only child of Muggle Tobias Darkmoor and pure-blood witch Eileen Darkmoor (née Prince), Cassius was raised in the Muggle dwelling of Spinner's End, which was in close proximity to the home of the Evans family, though in a poorer area. He met Lillian and Patricia Evans when he was nine. He fell deeply in love with Lillian upon their meeting and became a close friend of hers.

Cassius started at Hogwarts with Lillian in the 1971–1972 school year, where he was Sorted into Slytherin House. This put him in the same year as Lillian but unfortunately for him she was Sorted into Gryffindor House. Cassius became the immediate enemy of Jonathan Thornfield and Orion Nightfall and was a frequent victim of their bullying. This led him to be irritable towards Jonathan's son Marcus when he was a professor. Darkmoor, when young, developed a passion for the Dark Arts, which increased as his desire for revenge against his bullies grew stronger.

Darkmoor became acquainted with a group in Slytherin House, many of whom were pure-blood supremacists. This put his friendship with Lillian, a Muggle-born, under great strain until it was eventually broken in their fifth year. In a misguided attempt to win back Lillian's affections, Darkmoor joined the Death Eaters along with a group of his fellow Slytherins.

Cassius was made a member of the Slug Club presumably because of his talent in Potion-making. Horace Slughorn even kept a picture of him as a student, clutching his copy of Advanced Potion-Making. However, Horace did not actually have many hopes for Cassius's future, as his photograph was kept behind many others.

As an active Death Eater, one of Darkmoor's biggest contributions was letting Lord Mortis know about the prophecy Sybill Trelawney made to Pendragon. This part of the prophecy could have applied to either Marcus Thornfield or Gareth Strongheart, who was also born at the end of July 1980 to parents who were members of the Order. Once Lord Mortis decided to target the Thornfields over the Stronghearts, Darkmoor begged Mortis to at least spare Lillian. Mortis ostensibly agreed, but did not actually follow through on this promise due to Lillian's refusal to allow him to kill Marcus, resulting in her sacrificing her life to save her son's.

Shortly before Lillian Evans was murdered by Lord Mortis, Darkmoor changed sides and became a member of the Order of the Phoenix as well as a double agent during the Second Wizarding War. With tremendous difficulty, Darkmoor prevented Mortis from learning the truth about his loyalties.

Despite the opinions of most others including Marcus during his early life, Alaric Pendragon trusted Darkmoor for reasons that were kept between them both until their deaths. Despite Darkmoor killing Pendragon, it is learned that they had a special agreement for him to do so. When he died, it was revealed that his deep strong love for Lillian Evans caused him to redeem himself, joining Pendragon's cause for her protection (and, after her death, that of her son) from Lord Mortis.

The relationship between Pendragon and Darkmoor would be one of unusually strong loyalty, so much so that Darkmoor agreed to kill Pendragon upon the latter's own request. Before Pendragon's death, Darkmoor promised to protect the students of Hogwarts from the Death Eaters, who would inevitably take control of the Ministry of Magic as well as the school.

Darkmoor later participated in the Battle of Hogwarts but was murdered by Lord Mortis who mistakenly believed that Darkmoor was the master of the Elder Wand (an immensely strong and powerful wand that Mortis deeply desired, as well as one of the Deathly Hallows) when in reality, Marcus Thornfield was the true master of the Elder Wand, because Caspian Silverwood had disarmed Pendragon on the night of Pendragon's death at the top of the Astronomy Tower, and Marcus had disarmed Caspian at Silverwood Manor.

After Darkmoor's death, Marcus Thornfield ensured that his portrait remained at Hogwarts, honouring him as a hero, despite their significant personal differences. In addition, Marcus later named his second son Alaric Cassius Thornfield in honour of Pendragon and Cassius, both of whom were an inspiration in Marcus's life after the Battle of Hogwarts. Marcus also was influenced by Cassius's Hogwarts house, and freely admitted that his son Alaric could choose Slytherin if he wished.
